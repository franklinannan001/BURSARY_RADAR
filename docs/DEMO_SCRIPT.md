# Three-minute demo

1. Open the landing page and explain that the profile stays browser-first.
2. Select **Try as a sample student**. The sample is explicitly a first-year public university student in Machakos, Mwala.
3. Complete the five-step profile and show likely matches plus check-eligibility matches.
4. Explain that no opportunity listings are bundled. Create a government-official account with a `.go.ke` email, choose **Admin** at login, then submit a complete bursary at `/admin`.
5. Add a reviewed opportunity to My Plan and preview the 14-, 7- and 2-day SMS messages.
6. Open `/poll` for the projector poll and submit anonymous yes/no answers.
7. Log out, sign in as a student, and complete the matching flow using the newly added bursary.
